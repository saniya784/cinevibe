# 🎬 CineVibe

A mood-aware movie discovery platform that reshapes itself around how you want to feel.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [cinevibe-alpha.vercel.app](https://cinevibe-alpha.vercel.app)

---

## 📖 About

CineVibe is a React-based movie discovery app built as a learning project. Instead of scrolling through endless poster grids, you pick a **mood** — like *Mind-Bending*, *Emotional*, or *Adrenaline* — and the app reshapes its colors, recommendations, and featured films to match.

### What it does

- 🎨 **Mood-driven theming** — the whole interface shifts color based on the mood or movie you hover
- 🎞️ **Cinematic hero** — rotating featured films with a 3D poster stack
- 🎛️ **Vibe Match** — a 3-slider quiz that recommends one movie
- 🎭 **Mood Mixer** — blend two moods to find films that fit both
- 🔍 **Search** — press `Ctrl+K` for a command-palette search with live results
- ❤️ **Favorites, Watchlist, Ratings** — all saved in your browser
- 🌗 **Dark & Light themes**
- 🍿 **Cinema Mode** — dims everything except the movie
